# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Locals in Granollers (and nearby) who want artisan American-style baked goods delivered at home or for a special event. Primarily mobile users aged 18–40, browsing with cravings or a gifting intent. Secondary: event organizers and couples looking for catering (dessert tables, weddings, corporate).

They come hungry — they need to feel the product, not read about it. Decision is emotional and fast.

## Product Purpose

Beikit Bakery is an American bakery (cookies, cheesecakes, milkshakes, lattes) run by Juan & Anna in Granollers. The site exists to turn appetite into an order — via Uber Eats or WhatsApp — and catering inquiries into booked events. Success = a visitor who arrived curious leaves with an order placed or a WhatsApp conversation started.

## Brand Personality

Artesanal · Golosa · Cercana

Warm, handmade, unapologetically indulgent. The voice of a neighbor who bakes the best cookies on the block and happens to sell them. Not a chain, not a franchise — a project made with love by two real people.

## Anti-references

- Generic chain aesthetics (Starbucks, Dunkin', Krispy Kreme) — corporate polish, no soul
- Overly minimal Nordic/Scandinavian bakery look — cold, sterile, no appetite
- "Artisan bread" earthy-craft palette (linen, wheat, raw wood) — wrong register, wrong food category
- SaaS-cream beige landing pages — the warm-neutral AI default must be avoided in execution even if the brand bg is warm (the distinction is in richness, typography, and photography, not the hex)

## Design Principles

1. **Appetite before explanation.** Show the product first. Every section should make the viewer hungry before it informs them.
2. **Heartmade, not handcrafted-generic.** Warmth comes from specificity (Juan & Anna, Granollers, real recipes) not from warm colors and round fonts alone.
3. **Mobile-first cravings.** Most users are on a phone with a craving. The path from "I want this" to "order placed" must be frictionless and fast.
4. **Brand voice is the founders' voice.** Informal, direct, a little cheeky. Not corporate, not overly polished.
5. **Trust through specificity.** 48 Google reviews, +500 orders, real photos, real names — proof over claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Full keyboard navigation. Reduced-motion respect already implemented in CSS. Bilingual (ES/CA) support already in place.
