## Priority Issues

**[P1] Contrast failures — small text on cream and on orange**
GoogleRating text-dark/55 at 13px (~3.78:1), ClaimSection text-cream/70 at 11px (~1.64:1), ClaimSection tagline text-cream at 12px on orange (~3.13:1). Fix: darken opacity values, increase font weight.

**[P1] Em-dash overuse in Catering copy**
772 em-dashes in Catering.tsx. AI copy tell that undermines the artisanal brand voice.

**[P2] Bounce easing in two places**
RRSSSection.tsx:92 cubic-bezier(0.34,1.45,0.64,1) and ClaimSection.tsx:87 [0.34,1.56,0.64,1]. Replace with var(--ease-out) or Framer spring with bounce:0.
